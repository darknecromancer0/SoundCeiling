# v0.9.2 Samsung Media Auto Volume field acceptance

Install over v0.9.1/v0.8 with the same signer. Test the built-in speaker at low volume with a
conservative Safety Maximum. This is a field build, not physical safety certification.

1. Start allowed YouTube/Yandex playback with ordinary **Запустить**, not Relay.
2. Grant capture/source recognition. Exact targeted PCM, one active player, an allowed policy and
   a validated vendor Media curve are required. `reference_bounded` means both PRE/POST bounds
   agreed; `reference_ambiguous` deliberately holds.
3. Play alternating sustained quiet and loud sections. Expect one-step `media_auto_write` UP/DOWN,
   exact readback and `actuatorTier=SAMSUNG_MEDIA`. Short peaks may precede this reactive path.
4. Lower Safety Maximum. Automatic Media must never exceed it or jump to full volume.
5. During control press hardware Volume Down once. It must pass through and show a latched pause.
   For at least 20 seconds, change ceilings/strength and play loud/quiet sections: there must be no
   ordinary UP or DOWN. Volume Up must not resume. Only **Остановить → Запустить** may resume.
6. Repeat with Samsung's Media slider dragged down, including a key press at Media 0 when
   Accessibility is connected. Own app ACKs must not pause.
7. During pause, confirm Safety Maximum and explicit Quiet Now still work. Repeat Stop/Start five
   times and export one complete log.

Reject the build on any automatic write above hard cap, reversal of detected user down, ordinary
write after pause/Stop, mute/full-volume jump, Session DSP construction, or claimed audible Shadow.
Expected evidence: `media_auto_user_down_key`, `media_auto_authority`, `media_auto_write`,
`SAMSUNG_MEDIA_PAUSED`, exact source/capture state, bounds, cap and readbacks.
