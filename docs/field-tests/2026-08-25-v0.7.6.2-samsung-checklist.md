# SoundCeiling v0.7.6.2 Samsung field checklist

Target device: Samsung SM-A528B / Android 14. Repeat on the same Bluetooth route used for v0.7.6.1 when possible.

## 1. No Visualizer-driven volume sink

1. Start Smart PCM with Global DSP enabled and play stable material in Yandex Music or YouTube.
2. Put Samsung Media at a comfortable mid step such as 9/15 and leave the slider alone for at least 20 seconds.
3. Export the complete session log.

Pass:
- while capture reference is `UNKNOWN`, ordinary fallback reports `coarse_no_output_loudness` and does not emit `coarse_loudness_down` Media writes based only on Visualizer;
- a fresh Visualizer frame by itself never changes the control meter domain from `UNKNOWN` to `OUTPUT`;
- the Samsung user anchor does not slowly walk downward.

## 2. PRE_VOLUME projection wins

Continue playback until the log establishes `capture_reference ... PRE_VOLUME`.

Pass:
- control summaries switch to `meterDomain=PROJECTED` for the proven PRE_VOLUME source;
- `projectedOutputLoudness` and `projectedOutputPeak` include `mediaRouteGainDb` rather than copying the Visualizer level;
- changing Samsung Media changes the projected output in the same direction without relabeling the source as direct OUTPUT;
- no repeated 9→8→7→... sink occurs merely because the Visualizer level stays high.

## 3. Proven coarse fallback still works

Use material/settings that produce a sustained, genuinely above-ceiling projected output while DSP is unavailable.

Pass:
- ordinary fallback may lower Media by exactly one step after dwell;
- additional steps require new sustained evidence and dwell;
- recovery only repays SoundCeiling-owned attenuation debt and never exceeds the latest user anchor.

## 4. User authority

Manually test 1→2, 2→3, 3→2 and one larger downward move.

Pass:
- manual lowering is never immediately raised back;
- manual raising rebases the anchor instead of restoring an older SoundCeiling value;
- hard safety may still attenuate when its independent ceiling is actually violated.

## 5. Global DSP probe regression guard

The v0.7.6.1 safety sequence must remain intact:
`baseline with no DSP → neutral 0 dB attach → attach verification → bounded -0.5 dB differential probe`.

Pass:
- Visualizer remains available as paired evidence for the Global DSP verification path;
- unsafe/non-neutral attach is detached and may be suppressed for the route;
- an unverified transport is never reported as continuous verified DSP.

## 6. Lifecycle and evidence

- Stop/restart at least 5 times.
- Confirm no crash, forced mute, or slider fight.
- Export one complete log that includes the startup header and the relevant CONTROL/EVENT lines.
- Record the tested APK SHA-256.
