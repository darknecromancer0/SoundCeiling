# SoundCeiling v0.7.6.1 Samsung DSP safety checklist

Status: awaiting device test

Use only the APK built from the immutable v0.7.6.1 CI head and record its APK SHA-256 before installation.

## Physical acceptance cases

1. **Start with Media fixed at 1/15 or 2/15.** Playback must continue during the initial baseline. Before `dsp_global_attach_begin`, no session-zero DSP may be attached merely for diagnostics.
2. **Neutral attach phase.** The log must show `dsp_global_attach_begin` at `neutral_0db`, followed by `dsp_global_attach_result`. If the 0 dB attach itself mutes, strongly changes output, or loses the output meter, SoundCeiling must log `dsp_global_attach_unsafe`, detach the effect, and suppress further route probes.
3. **Small differential probe.** Only after a safe neutral attach may the log show `dsp_differential_probe_begin` with `requestedGainDb=-0.5`. A strong response to -0.5 dB must be classified `RESPONSIVE_NONLINEAR`, not mistaken for `NO_EFFECT`, and must not authorize continuous Global DSP.
4. **No repeated thumps.** After an unsafe attach/probe, the same route must remain under `dsp_global_probe_suppressed` until the route changes or Global DSP is toggled. The app must not repeatedly attach/detach every few seconds.
5. **If verification succeeds**, keep Samsung Media fixed and compare loud/quiet passages. `dspAppliedGainDb` should move while the Samsung slider stays at the user anchor.
6. **Manual authority regression:** perform 1→2, 2→3 and 3→2. SoundCeiling must not snap the slider back. Hard cap and Quiet Now remain downward-only safety paths.
7. **Stop/restart 5 times.** No crash, cache warning, stale attenuation, forced Media reset, or stuck session-zero effect is acceptable.
8. **Log retention regression.** Generate several sessions, reopen the logs list, and confirm logs from the last 24 hours remain available. The normal retained budget is now 64 MiB.
9. Export one complete log containing startup, neutral attach, differential probe, loud/quiet playback, manual Media moves, and final stop.
