# SoundCeiling v0.8.0 Samsung field checklist

Target device: Samsung SM-A528B / Android 14. This is a bounded compatibility pilot, not final
1.0 acceptance. Preserve the complete exported log even when the first custom candidate fails.

## 1. Install and setup

1. Record the Git commit, workflow run, APK SHA-256 and signer SHA-256. The expected development
   signer remains `5AA109027B8AE7675CE543EAF26402A2890BCA97510BC2018661EA2231516BE2`.
2. Install over v0.7.7.10 so persisted settings and stable signing continuity are exercised.
3. Grant Enhanced Session discovery once if needed:
   `adb shell pm grant dev.soundceiling.app android.permission.DUMP`.
4. Confirm Yandex Music is allowed, Global DSP is enabled, targeted playback capture is active,
   and Strict Safety accessibility is connected.
5. Set Safety Maximum to 3/15 for the hard-cap check, then leave Samsung Media at **Media 3/15**
   for the normalization check.

## 2. Candidate matrix authority

Play steady Yandex Music material and wait at least 15 seconds.

Expected log sequence:

- exact source identity and non-zero session ownership are present;
- one or more `session_dsp_candidate_attempt` records appear in documented order;
- every failed attempt has `session_dsp_candidate_result ... verified=false` before the next attempt;
- at most one `session_dsp_candidate_selected` appears;
- selected topology is one of `cts_frequency_full_bypass_stereo`,
  `frequency_limiter_bypass_stereo`, or `frequency_input_gain_only_stereo`;
- no OEM-default/fallback candidate appears anywhere.

If the matrix is exhausted, the acceptable result is `enhanced_session_matrix_exhausted` followed
by HOLD/`session_dsp_unavailable`. Stop the test and export the log; do not raise Samsung Media to
compensate and do not retry an unchanged suppressed session.

## 3. Bounded real gain at Media 3/15

With a selected candidate, keep the Samsung slider untouched at Media 3/15 and play a quiet section
for at least 30 seconds, then a clearly louder section.

Pass conditions:

- `session_dsp_apply` shows real non-zero applied gain;
- positive applied gain never exceeds `+3.00 dB`, even if requested gain is larger;
- the quiet section moves applied DSP gain upward and the loud section moves it down/negative;
- ordinary control summaries remain `actuatorTier=SESSION_DSP` and Samsung Media stays 3/15;
- no ordinary `coarse_media_write` occurs and no user anchor is synthesized by DSP activity;
- projected output peak does not exceed the configured hard peak ceiling.

Immediately stop and export the log if the phone output jumps toward maximum volume, becomes
asymmetric, distorts, or the Samsung slider moves without a user/safety action.

## 4. Output anomaly guard

Review all positive-gain intervals. There must be no unexplained near-full-scale Visualizer output.
If `enhanced_session_output_anomaly` appears, verify that it is immediately followed by
`session_output_guard_suppressed`, neutralization/release, and HOLD. Applied DSP gain must return to
0 dB and the same session must not reattach until session or route identity changes. A guard trip is
a safe rejection of that device/profile, not a successful compatibility result.

## 5. User authority and Strict Safety non-regression

1. Press Volume-Down once. It must pass through immediately, become the new user anchor, and must
   not be raised back except repayment of earlier SoundCeiling-owned attenuation debt.
2. Press Volume-Up back to the legal maximum. The accessibility path may apply the legal next step.
3. Hold Volume-Up at the maximum. Media must not persist above the cap.
4. Drag the Samsung panel repeatedly above the maximum for 20 seconds. Reactive corrections may
   race SystemUI briefly, but illegal observations must be logged as `hard_cap_overshoot_rejected`
   and must never update the Media anchor or Linked Lock ceilings.
5. Press Volume-Down during/after the burst. It must not be counter-written upward.

## 6. Lifecycle cleanup

For each action below, confirm a neutralize/release before any later attach:

- stop SoundCeiling while gain is non-zero;
- start again;
- switch speaker/headphones or another available route;
- replace playback capture permission/session;
- change the allowed-app policy;
- switch from Yandex Music to another source and back.

No stale verification epoch may attach after stop, and no non-zero gain may survive a route,
capture, policy or session identity change.

## 7. Export

Export the full SoundCeiling log after all steps. Record audible observations, exact test times,
selected profile, maximum positive and negative applied gain, whether Media ever visibly exceeded
the cap, and whether any output guard fired. Do not trim the startup, candidate failures, slider
burst or stop/restart sections.
